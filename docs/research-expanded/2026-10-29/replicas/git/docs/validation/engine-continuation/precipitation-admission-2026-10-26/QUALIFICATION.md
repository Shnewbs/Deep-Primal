# Follow-up qualification — 2026-10-26

The complete staged core suite now passes with a 768MiB heap and a600-second
deadline. Earlier interrupted/180-second attempts remain historical failures;
they are not deleted or rewritten. Native fixture compilation passes through
Gradle (14 tasks,3 executed). This is not a fresh full Gradle build.

Added `PrecipitationPersistenceChecks.java`, a private disposable-world fixture.
It invokes the actual private precipitation method, proves nonzero demand with
an admitted control (131072 quanta), then checks three unchanged-state rejections
against saturated ground/snow stores. A fresh session and a separate process
reopen retain exact water/salt/sediment, with codec round trips, explicit pass
records, save-flush acknowledgments and completed shutdowns. The remote aquifer
does not load its corresponding chunk. Disable background environmental updates
only inside this opt-in fixture to keep unrelated transfers out of its totals.

The identical fixture against the unpatched dev.28 JAR crashes with the expected
IllegalStateException from WaterAccount.distribute through HydrologyRuntime's
precipitation method. Gradle exits0; the harness correctly exits1 for missing
acceptance. The baseline crash and harness failure are retained.

The candidate is a **diagnostic-only** dev.28 archive with independently compiled
staged WaterAccount/Parcel classes substituted in its nested core. It is not a
new release or a full current-main build, and is not offered for download. Its
identity and both native receipts are retained. Exact source is dev.28 d97cb34
plus the staged patch, without the new cliff renderer. No old binary is relabeled
as a newly qualified production build.

Concurrent foreground cliff commit6cd9971 was read and preserved. Its two changed
core/test sources were compiled with the staged accounting classes in separate
outputs; cliff wall/ledge geometry and all accounting regressions pass together.
This combined CPU check does not rerun foreground GPU receipts or certify the
combined mod. Art-candidate commitd6c9674 is preserved. The independent audit
remains the0653 report pinned0818658, last complete grade5.0/10.

Next gate: restore the complete current-main source inputs (including cliff
rendering), promote only after a uniquely versioned full Gradle/module/package
build and current-artifact native checks. Only754MiB remained on this host after
these isolated checks; disk headroom must be reconciled before another full
build. No claim is made that a build was attempted and failed for disk reasons.
Full water ledger/unload ownership, broad visuals, multiplayer/soak, V8 and named
hardware performance remain open.

Fixture environment: DEEPPRIMAL_PRECIPITATION_RECORD=<record-path> and
DEEPPRIMAL_PRECIPITATION_MODE=fresh or reopen. Use separate disposable worlds,
packagedValidation and visualValidation; require the mode-specific pass record.
It intentionally writes a local fixture surface and account state. Never enable
it in an owner save. Keep all fixture classes out of released archives.
