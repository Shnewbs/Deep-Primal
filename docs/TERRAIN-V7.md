# Terrain V7 — alpha.28

V7 addresses the owner's report of narrow streams, similar-looking biomes and similar terrain across seeds. Create a **new normal world** with the built-in engine enabled to use `deepprimal:continental_v7`. Standard remains Y=-128..639; Deep remains Y=-384..639. Existing V1–V6 worlds retain their saved generator, terrain profile, water amounts and player edits, including newly explored chunks in those worlds. They do not silently switch to V7. V7 geography is frozen after this release; further geographic changes require a new saved revision.

## Changes

The previous gradient fields sampled an integer lattice at the world origin. For eight tested seeds, V6's raw height, rain and temperature at (0,0) were identical: 165.42108, 0.63967 and 0.80483. This was a real local seed-aliasing defect; the whole previous world was not seed-independent. V7 adds a deterministic, seed-derived sampling phase. All eight origins now differ in raw height and rainfall, and actual saved-world/UI-entered seed binding is checked in the packaged game.

V7 uses continuous regional blends between lowlands, rolling hills, plateau rims and folded mountain ridges. Seed-varied continent lobes sit inside drainage domains with an ocean margin. Climate has a seed-varied equator, broader rainfall variation and elevation cooling. These are authored controls on the existing terrain/drainage architecture, not a claim of complete geological simulation.

Channel radius grows faster with contributing area. Across the fixed eight-seed central-domain ensemble, median planned channel width increases from approximately 2.8–2.9 blocks to 5.1–5.8 blocks; larger reaches grow further. Width here is twice the continuous plan radius, not a guarantee that every rasterized or partly drained water cross-section has that many wet blocks. Headwater tips and finite streams can still be narrow, shrink or dry out. The new width/depth is established during generation, with no perpetual runtime refill.

Canopy density follows the selected biome. Forest/jungle trees can root on modest slopes without requiring the entire canopy footprint to be flat. Plains retain sparse trees. Badlands use red sand and shallow terracotta layers; wetlands gain mud horizons, dark forests podzol, and biome ground cover includes ferns, meadow flowers and dead bushes. Exposed slopes already use folded/faulted province-based rock beds, while the material profile separates topsoil, subsoil and weathered parent material by climate, sediment and slope. Material revision 3 varies outcrop band thickness, lateral bedding and facies, assigning broad sedimentary, igneous and metamorphic provinces throughout the rock column. Revision 4 confines those bands to steep highlands (surface elevation ≥150, slope >1.3) and their upper 96 rock blocks. Revision 5 additionally preserves the original granite and basalt intrusive bodies within those exposed bands. Revision 6 adds a spatially coherent sand-to-soil shoreline ecotone: sand propensity varies continuously with ground height and rainfall, and intermediate columns receive sandy-loam soil instead of a speckled per-column sand decision. Existing Minecraft stone blocks are visual proxies rather than a precise petrology catalog. Only new V7 worlds saved with the corresponding material revision use that mapping; revisions 0–5 and existing saves retain their prior behavior. Sand uses a climate colormap, now softly blending humid sand toward the biome's grass tint while retaining its dry sand palette. Existing Minecraft biome/block assets remain temporary palettes. This is not a completed species/content catalog.

V7 inherits V6's saved creation-time profile, geological caves, river tunnels, deposits, glaciers, finite melt, dunes and bounded cave-ins. See [V6 mechanics and configuration](TERRAIN-V6.md). No deposit default, transport budget or recharge law is changed in this release.

## Channel revisions 3–8

Channel revision 3 adds river-mouth delta fans; revision 4 adds oxbow lakes on top; revision 5 gives each fan's distributaries conserved discharge shares and sqrt(share) widths. Revision 6 combines those features with deeper main channels. Revision 7 applies the deeper main-channel cross-section to the earlier V7 channel plan without adding delta fans, oxbows or distributary branches. The standard and deep presets select integrated revision 9; saved revisions remain available through the codec (0..9), and revisions 0–8 retain their prior geometry meaning.

Revision 8 introduced the previously integrated default. It retains revision 6's river/delta/oxbow/distributary plan and deep main channels, and deepens still-water lake basins and oxbow beds without changing their footprints, water coverage, surface stages or route planning. The standard and deep presets also select material revision 6. Biome tags enable additional shoreline cover in swamp, mangrove, river and jungle biomes and mangrove trees near water in swamp/mangrove biomes. Tree trunks remain on dry bank columns; underwater vegetation and changes to custom water blocks are intentionally excluded.

Where a routed trunk with at least 1,800,000 blocks of catchment reaches the ocean with its water surface no more than six blocks above sea level, the generator plans a sediment fan (at most six per 8192-block region, largest first). The fan surface rises gently inland from sea level + 0.35 to the mouth stage + 0.45, and only raises ground below that surface; it never lowers or buries higher terrain. Three distributary reaches fork from the trunk at 45% of the fan and spread to about 62% of its half-width, ending just above sea level with roughly 1–3 blocks of water. Low ground, shallow estuary and shoals inside the fan become a flat delta plain; deeper water and the open ocean are left alone.

Revision 4 adds at most 24 oxbow lakes per 8192-block region beside flat, unincised lowland trunks (catchment ≥ 160,000, incision ≤ 0.5, braid ≤ 0.05, grade ≤ 0.0035, stage ≥ sea + 1.5). Each cut-off is a tapering crescent whose still surface sits at least 1.2 blocks below the lowest sampled rim; the checks require the sampled lakes to stay within 3 blocks of the parent river stage (measured 2.25). Candidates near channels, deltas, lakes, the sea or the region edge are rejected, as are loops with more than 8 blocks of rim relief or a rim more than 9 blocks above the reach. The bed is bowl-shaped, flow is zero, and the surrounding bank rises to the lake level + 0.35. Oxbows are indexed with the existing channel/delta tiles and only rework their own footprint plus bank band.

Revision 5 splits each fan's trunk discharge between its three distributary branches instead of drawing them as equal copies. A deterministic field gives the middle branch the largest share (0.49 in the checked corpus), the side branches less (down to 0.24), and the three shares sum to one; no branch gains water the trunk does not carry. Branch width follows sqrt(share) around the revision-4 capacity-fair width, so the dominant branch is the widest (measured widest/narrowest 1.27) and its bed is correspondingly deeper through the hydraulic-geometry depth rule. Branch mouths must stay wet, seaward-flowing and within the delta depth limit, and nothing outside the fan footprint changes; revisions 3 and 4 replay exactly against frozen digests.

Revisions 6–8 deepen non-delta, non-lake Earth-river cross-sections according to channel radius (up to 20 blocks before braid adjustment), without changing routed centerlines, water stages, widths or ground outside the channels. The deeper bed is still bounded by the existing grade-aware cascade floor; braided reaches retain their braid depth reduction. Revisions 6 and 7 do not deepen still-water lakes, delta branches, oceans or preexisting worlds. Revision 6 includes revisions 3–5's delta/oxbow/distributary features; revision 7 is depth-only. Revision 8 combines the revision-6 channel features with lake and oxbow bed deepening. Existing saved revisions remain frozen.

Revision 8 was the previous default for new standard/deep V7 presets. It deepens still-water lakes and oxbows, but not delta branches, oceans or preexisting worlds; ocean trenches remain governed by their separate terrain profile. Full core checks pass: the revision-6/8 comparison sampled 1,502 lakes (941 deepened, max 16.12 blocks) and 15 oxbows (all deepened, max 1.6 blocks), while preserving basin planning and water levels. Revision 7 remains the depth-only channel option. The material-6 ecotone regression passes with 129 sand and 895 loam cells in 1,024 samples and 1,920/1,984 matching neighboring classifications. Visual-validation sources compile; generated-world save/reopen and visual acceptance remain open.

Limitations: geography remains frozen, so the fan does not prograde into `mask < 0` ocean; there is no avulsion or anastomosing behaviour, oxbows are static cut-offs rather than simulated meander migration, and the revision-5 budget is planned hydraulic geometry rather than a routed discharge state or a time-varying allocation. Braided reaches still use a single implicit discharge. Revision 8 does not alter ocean bathymetry or place underwater plants; tagged trees are bank-rooted. Native generated-world save/reopen and comparative visual acceptance for revisions 3–8 remain open.

## Water rendering, including existing saves

A corner average could pull a thin upper water layer below its solid bed when its downhill neighbor occupied the layer below. The mesher now applies a shared-corner support constraint, keeping a supported film at least 1/256 block above its floor. Both animated water and the smooth fallback use it. The shared-corner rule preserves independently meshed page agreement; it does not add water, place sources, alter the riverbed or prevent real drainage.

Matched native stair fixtures contain the same 325 units (thin) or 3,900 units (fuller), identical block-state hashes and plains biome in alpha.27 and alpha.28. The previous package shows clipped dry-looking strips; the candidate exposes the supported thin surface. This diagnoses one cause of the reported appearance, not every possible water artifact. Small visual flecks, transparent overlaps, distant page/LOD coverage and temporal behavior across broad editable worlds still need separate acceptance. A static frozen fixture is not a live hydrology endurance test.

## Compatibility and limits

The release retains finite environmental water accounting. Streams may exhaust; rainfall/recharge can feed suitable locations elsewhere under the existing rules. There is no unlimited source conversion or flood-to-fill operation. Installing the JAR does not widen rivers in old worlds or regenerate player edits.

[Validation](VALIDATION-0.0.1-alpha.28.md) records the exact packages, native tests and rejected runs. These checks cover eight core seeds, four native seeds, Standard/Deep save/reload, an actual Create World flow and software OpenGL/Vulkan views. They do not establish every-seed quality, long-duration storm/drought balance, large-region hydraulic continuation, finished LOD/transparency, Windows GPU execution or FPS guarantees. The mountain view is limited by the short test render distance; the river view includes a broad mouth. Those screenshots are illustrative, not comprehensive landscape acceptance.

## Natural shoreline pass — channel revision 9

New authored engine profiles select revision 9. It retains revision-8 routing, lake plans and water stages. Coherent shallow-margin deposits can retreat the wet outline inside the former footprint; protected inlet/outlet corridors remain wet, and the pass never expands water outside the existing basin. Within those admitted lakes it varies shelf width and pool relief with coherent world-space fields, adds bounded dry-bank height variation, and uses transport/deposition conditions plus coherent patches to distinguish sand, silt, clay and gravel beds. The native wet-soil path previously collapsed most non-clay/non-gravel beds to sand.

Eligible dry banks gain clustered canopy admission and denser, patchy ground cover through the owned `deepprimal:riparian_mosaic` tag. The low-elevation exclusion is relaxed only for eligible dry banks near actual planned water. Runtime water-unit accounting and transport are unchanged; the new saved generation revision changes initial terrain and its corresponding initial water coverage. Bed facies are computed once per column rather than per soil voxel.

The submerged/floating/rooted plant branches still require a separate fluid-compatible implementation: their vanilla-water guards do not admit engine-water blocks, and replacing engine water with native waterlogged plants would lose the independent volume contract. This pass does not claim aquatic plants, a finished ecosystem, an in-game visual pass or a new release JAR.

See [measured comparison and validation](validation/natural-shores/RESULTS.md). Existing saved revisions remain readable; no retrogen is applied.
